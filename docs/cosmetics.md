# Cosmetics

FLOW cosmetics are exclusive to the FLOW platform.

They are not Steam items. They are not tradeable. They are not owned outside the platform.

They are access-based cosmetics that work while playing on FLOW.

## Goals

The cosmetics system should:

- Give players something useful to spend credits on.
- Reward people who play and contribute to the platform.
- Avoid gambling-style mechanics.
- Avoid tradeable markets at launch.
- Keep everything inside FLOW.
- Allow custom FLOW-only items that do not exist in CS2.
- Support cheaper restricted skin packs and more expensive individual cosmetics.

## Platform-only access

When a player unlocks a FLOW cosmetic, they get access to it on the FLOW platform.

They do not receive a Steam inventory item.

This means:

- No trading at launch
- No external ownership
- No cash-out
- No speculation
- No skin gambling

Credits are earned on FLOW, and cosmetics are used on FLOW.

## Skin packs

Skin packs are bundled cosmetic loadouts.

A skin pack gives access to multiple cosmetics at a lower total price, but it is restricted.

Rules:

- You equip the whole pack.
- You cannot equip only one skin from the pack.
- You cannot mix individual items from the pack with other cosmetics.
- You can switch between owned packs.
- Packs are cheaper than buying individual cosmetics separately.
- Pack items do not become individually owned cosmetics.

This gives players a cheaper way to get a full look, while individual cosmetics remain more flexible and more expensive.

## Custom skins

FLOW can include custom skins that do not exist in CS2.

For example:

- Custom knife finishes
- Custom weapon skins
- FLOW-branded skins
- Seasonal skins
- Ranked reward skins
- Server-host reward skins
- Clan reward skins
- Tournament reward skins

These would only be available while playing on FLOW servers that support the cosmetics system.

## Community skins

Community-made skins may be added later.

This has not been decided yet.

If community skins are added, FLOW should have clear rules for:

- Who made the skin
- Whether it can be used on the platform
- Whether the creator is credited
- Whether the creator receives platform rewards
- Whether the skin fits the FLOW style
- Whether it creates legal or copyright issues

## Cosmetic rarity

Cosmetics may have rarity tiers.

| Tier              | Notes                                     |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Common            | Easy to unlock                            |
| Uncommon          | Slightly more expensive                   |
| Rare              | Requires more credits or special activity |
| Epic              | Harder to get                             |
| Legendary         | Very expensive or event-based             |
| Seasonal          | Available during a specific season        |
| Host reward       | Earned by hosting good servers            |
| Clan reward       | Earned from clan activity                 |
| Tournament reward | Earned from tournaments                   |

Rarity should be used carefully. The goal is to make cosmetics interesting, not predatory.

## Unlock methods

Cosmetics could be unlocked through:

- Credits
- Skin packs
- Match milestones
- Seasonal rewards
- Server hosting rewards
- Reputation rewards
- Clan rewards
- Tournament prizes
- Event participation
- Admin grants for special cases

Most cosmetics should probably be credit-based, since credits are the main reward system.

## Marketplace

A player marketplace may be considered later, but it is not part of the initial FLOW economy.

A marketplace could allow players to trade or sell platform cosmetics, similar to community trading systems.

This is not decided yet.

Reasons to delay the marketplace:

- It adds moderation complexity.
- It can encourage farming and abuse.
- It makes cosmetics feel like financial assets.
- It creates pricing and speculation problems.
- It may conflict with the simple platform-only cosmetics model.

For now, FLOW cosmetics should be earned and spent inside the platform, not traded between players.

## No gambling

FLOW cosmetics should not use gambling mechanics.

Avoid:

- Loot boxes
- Paid random rolls
- Cash-out systems
- Tradeable gambling value
- Anything that turns cosmetics into financial assets

FLOW cosmetics should be simple platform rewards.

## Notes

Cosmetics are one of the main credit sinks in FLOW.

The goal is to make playing, behaving well, and hosting good servers feel rewarding without creating a marketplace or external economy.
